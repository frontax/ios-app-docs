# Privacy Policy

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the iOS app "Tozan Kiroku" (Mountain Climbing Record, hereinafter "the App") handles user information.
The App respects user privacy and is committed to protecting personal information.

## 1. Information Collected

The App collects the following information:

- Location data (used for GPS tracking)
- Photo library access (used for attaching photos to hiking records and for the photo scan feature to auto-detect past hikes)
- File access (used for importing GPX files, photos, and backup files)
- Microphone access (used for recording voice memos)
- Speech recognition access (used for transcribing voice memos)
- Hiking-related data (mountain names, dates, elevation, distance, notes, course time plans and actuals, etc.)

## 2. Data Storage

All data is stored exclusively on your device.
No data is transmitted to external servers.
App data is included in device backups (iCloud or Mac/PC), enabling restoration when switching devices.
The database and image files are protected by the device's encryption features.
Exported files (GPX, CSV, backup archives) are also protected by device encryption.
Auto-backup data recorded during hikes (GPS track points and session information) is stored in the app's private area on the device and is automatically deleted when the hike is completed or canceled.

## 3. Handling of Multilingual Support

The App supports display in both Japanese and English.
Language switching is performed automatically based on your device's system settings, and no information about language preferences is transmitted externally.
All localized display data such as mountain names and prefecture names is contained within the app, and no external translation services are contacted.

## 4. Handling of Statistics

The App calculates statistics from hiking records (monthly/yearly hike counts, cumulative elevation, walking distance, prefecture distribution of summited mountains, summit completion rates for all mountain categories, personal best records, etc.).
Progress information displayed on highlight cards, such as the Famous Mountains summit completion rate, is also calculated from hiking record data stored on the device.
All statistics are calculated and displayed from hiking record data on the device and are never transmitted to external servers.

## 5. Handling of Course Time Information

The App records and manages course time plans (estimated times and rest stops for each checkpoint) and actuals (actual passage times).
All course time calculations (estimated duration based on trail distance and elevation gain) are performed on the device.
Course time information is stored only on the device and is never transmitted to external servers.

## 6. Handling of Photos

Photos saved in the App have their embedded location data (EXIF GPS information) automatically removed for privacy protection.
Other metadata such as capture date and time is retained.
The photo scan feature analyzes the location data and capture times of photos in your photo library on the device to auto-detect hiking records to famous mountains. This analysis is performed entirely on the device, and photo data and location information are never transmitted to external servers. Photos registered as hiking records from scan results are saved with location data removed.

## 7. Handling of Voice Memos and Transcription

The App records voice memos during hikes and performs transcription on the device.
All recording data and transcription results are stored only on the device.
Speech recognition prioritizes on-device processing. Apple's speech recognition servers are used only when the device does not support on-device recognition. In that case, audio data is processed in accordance with Apple's Privacy Policy.
Recording data is automatically deleted from the device after transcription is complete.

## 8. Handling of AI Features (Highlight Card Post Generation)

The App uses Apple Intelligence to automatically generate social media posts from hiking records.
All AI processing is performed on the device (on-device), and hiking record data is never transmitted to external servers.
Requests (instruction text) entered by users during AI regeneration are also processed only on the device and are never transmitted externally.
Generated text is reviewed and edited by the user before sharing.
On devices that do not support Apple Intelligence, template-based text generation is used.

## 9. Handling of Backup and Restore

Backup files created by the App's backup feature contain hiking record data (including course time plans and actuals) and attached images.
Backup files are stored on the device and are not transmitted externally unless the user explicitly performs a sharing action.
Backup files include hash values (SHA-256) for data integrity verification, detecting tampering or corruption during restoration.
Users are responsible for storing and sharing backup files.

## 10. Lock Screen and Dynamic Island Display

The App displays hiking information such as walking distance, elevation, and elapsed time on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island during hikes (Live Activity feature).
Displayed information is processed on the device and is never transmitted to external servers.
The display is automatically cleared when the hike ends or is canceled.

## 11. Handling of In-App Events

The App may offer limited-time in-app events (e.g., Summer Mountain Challenge).
All event period determination and changes to free version record limits are performed on the device, with no external server communication.
Badge and other information associated with events is also stored only on the device.

## 12. Handling of Review Requests

The App may display an App Store review request dialog using iOS standard functionality (StoreKit) when certain conditions are met.
All review request display decisions (version, display interval) are made on the device, with no external server communication.
Reviews are submitted through Apple's App Store, and the App does not collect or store review content.

## 13. Social Media Sharing

Social media sharing of highlight cards and posts is performed only when the user explicitly initiates a sharing action.
The handling of information on the destination social media services is governed by each service's privacy policy.

## 14. Disclosure to Third Parties

User personal information is never provided, sold, or shared with third parties.

## 15. Communication with External Services

The App communicates with the following external services.
No user personal information is transmitted to any of these services.

- Geospatial Information Authority of Japan (GSI) Tile Server (downloading map tile images)
- GSI Elevation API (obtaining trail elevation profiles)
- Open-Meteo API (obtaining mountain weather forecast data and sunset times)
- Overpass API / OpenStreetMap (searching for hiking trails and approach roads)

Communications include the range of user location information (latitude/longitude ranges), but no device-identifying information or account information is transmitted.
All communications are conducted over HTTPS (encrypted communication).
A timeout of 15 seconds is set for all communications, preventing unresponsive connections from being maintained for extended periods.

## 16. Audio Output

The App provides spoken warnings when route deviation is detected.
All audio is generated on the device, with no external server communication.

## 17. In-App Purchases

In-app purchases (Pro version) are processed through Apple's payment system.
Payment information (credit card numbers, etc.) is never collected or stored by the App.
Purchase status is securely stored in the device's Keychain (secure storage).

## 18. Data Deletion

Deleting the App from your device removes all stored data (including hiking records, course times, source data for statistics, and voice memo transcription data).
Individual hiking records can also be deleted from within the App.
Deleting a hiking record automatically recalculates statistics based on remaining records.
Auto-backup data from hikes is deleted upon hike completion, cancellation, or dismissal from the recovery dialog.
Data exported as backup files must be manually deleted by the user from the Files app or similar.

## 19. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be changed without prior notice.
Changes become effective upon publication on this page.
